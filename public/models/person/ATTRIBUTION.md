# Civilian driver character attribution

The current playable character is the original Blender-customized **Remy**, an Adobe Mixamo human model, exported as **person.glb**. It uses the source's texture-mapped torso, skin, hair, eyes, clothing and footwear. Its customization adds a charcoal shirt, olive cargo shorts, matte PBR surface treatment, in-place retargeted movement, and authored driving/vehicle entry/exit poses. The saved scanned-head variant is credited below.

- Original character: [Adobe Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/), Remy.
- Downloaded FBX source mirror: [GbotHQ/mixamo-characters — Remy.fbx](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GbotHQ/mixamo-characters/blob/main/Remy.fbx).
- Idle, Walk and Run motion reference: [Three.js Soldier.glb](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb), originally Adobe Mixamo. The source character itself is not included in the game.
- Adobe's [Mixamo FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html) permits royalty-free character and animation use in personal, commercial and non-profit video-game projects. This file is part of the game; no separate resale/redistribution license for standalone source assets is claimed.
- The source reference's Three.js MIT notice is retained in `THREEJS-LICENSE.txt`.

The original source files and editable packed Blender scene are retained locally under `artifacts/blender/person*`. Rebuild with Blender 5.2 using `scripts/build-blender-person.py`. The shipped compact model is `person.glb`; technical dimensions, bones and clip durations are listed in `person-manifest.json`.

The playable winter variant, `person-winter.glb`, retains this character's face, original hairstyle, skeleton, and six animations. Its fitted pearl-and-silver insulated suit, thermal neck liner, technical panels, gloves, winter trousers, and boots are authored for this game with `scripts/build-player-winter.py`. Garment textures, quilting, seams, ice-blue lighting accents, and hardware are generated locally. Its editable scene and preview renders are retained under `artifacts/blender/person-winter*`; the exported asset details are recorded in `person-winter-manifest.json`.

The saved alternative character, **person-realistic.glb**, is a Blender-authored derivative combining the compatible Mixamo body and skeleton with **Infinite, 3D Head Scan** by **Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The original notice is retained in [LEE-PERRY-SMITH-LICENSE.txt](./LEE-PERRY-SMITH-LICENSE.txt). The head mesh, scanned albedo and tangent normal were obtained from the [official Three.js example assets](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith). Original work: [Infinite Realities](http://www.ir-ltd.net/) / [Triplegangers](https://www.triplegangers.com/).

Changes for this game include fitting and skinning the scan to the existing head/neck, eye detailing, fair skin materials with varied roughness, a dark olive sleeveless cotton top, bound garment openings, fuller arm anatomy, charcoal cargo trousers, and dark lace-up footwear. The walking and running arms are reauthored as reciprocal civilian swings; the captured legs and vehicle animation contract are retained. Texture and geometry modifications are derivatives, with no suggestion of endorsement by the original creators.

Rebuild with `scripts/build-player-realistic.py` in Blender 5.2. It reads the existing packed `artifacts/blender/person.blend` and the downloaded scan files under `artifacts/blender/realistic-source/` (`head.glb`, `head-albedo.jpg`, `head-normal.jpg`, and `LICENSE.txt`, using the source filenames `LeePerrySmith.glb`, `Map-COL.jpg`, `Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg`, and `LeePerrySmith_License.txt`). It writes `person-realistic.glb`, `person-realistic-manifest.json`, an editable packed `artifacts/blender/person-realistic.blend`, and studio previews. Canonical and winter source assets remain available.
